## What should work afterwards?
The material **Gypsum plaster** exists in our model, with the values we need later for mass, heat-loss and CO₂ calculations.

## Plan
Create the file `data/materials/MAT-PLASTER.json`:

```json
{
  "id": "MAT-PLASTER",
  "name": "Gypsum plaster",
  "density_kg_m3": <number>,
  "lambda_w_mk": <number>,
  "source": "<where your values come from>"
}
```

- Research the **density** (kg/m³) and the **thermal conductivity λ** (W/(m·K)), e.g. in DIN 4108-4, in ÖKOBAUDAT (oekobaudat.de) or in a manufacturer's data sheet.
- Write your source into `source`.
- Optional: `gwp_kgco2e_per_kg` from ÖKOBAUDAT (modules A1–A3, converted to per kg).
